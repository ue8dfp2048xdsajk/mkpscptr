1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to website owners about how visitors use the site.

Cookies can be first-party (set by the website you are visiting) or third-party (set by a different domain, typically for analytics or payment services). They can be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (remain on your device for a set period).

4. How to Manage Cookies

Using our consent banner

When you first visit Mockup Rabbit, a consent banner appears at the bottom of the page. You can click Accept to allow analytics cookies or Decline to reject them. Strictly necessary cookies (Clerk auth and Stripe) are always active as they are required for the service to function.

Your preference is saved in the ms_cookie_consent cookie. To change your preference, clear your browser's cookies for this site and reload the page - the banner will reappear.

Using your browser settings

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Instructions for popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies (such as Clerk authentication cookies) will prevent you from signing in to your account.

Opting out of PostHog analytics

If you accepted analytics cookies and later wish to opt out, you can decline cookies in your browser settings for this site, which will prevent PostHog from collecting data on future visits. You may also submit an opt-out request by emailing support@mockuprabbit.com.
