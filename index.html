FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still have questions? Email us at support@mockuprabbit.com

What is Mockup Rabbit? ▾

Mockup Rabbit is a browser-based bulk mockup editor built for eCommerce, POD (Print-On-Demand) and digital product sellers. Upload backgrounds and designs, build every combo in one grid, batch-edit 100+ mockups at once, and export when you’re on a paid plan - no desktop software to install.

Is there a free plan? ▾

Yes. The free plan gives you full access to the bulk editor - unlimited mockups, batch editing, and all effects (including Pro effects) to try. Your preview is watermarked on mockups with designs. Export, cloud save, and local JSON save/load require a paid plan (Starter or Pro).

What’s the difference between Starter and Pro? ▾

Both require a free account to export. Starter includes local JSON save/load, exports flat-lay mockups without PRO effects and watermarks, exports canvas text as a .txt file, and includes one cloud-saved project. Mockups using Pro & 3D effects (warp, perspective, patterns, advanced blends, and more) need Pro to export - or you can skip those windows and export the rest. Pro unlocks exporting all mockups clean, the full photorealistic toolkit, pattern PNG export, up to 50 cloud projects, Save as New, and priority support.

Do I need design skills or Photoshop? ▾

No. Drag backgrounds and designs into the grid, batch-edit your selection, and export on a paid plan. No Photoshop, no install, and no credit card required to try the full editor.

What’s the difference between local save and cloud save? ▾

Local save (JSON) downloads your full project to your device - great for backups or moving work between computers manually; it requires Starter or Pro. Cloud save stores your project on our servers so you can pick up where you left off from any browser; Starter includes one cloud project, Pro up to 50 plus Save as New. Cloud save requires sign-in.

Where did my export go? ▾

ZIP (default) saves mockups.zip to your browser’s download location - usually the Downloads folder on Mac, Windows, and Linux (unless you changed that in browser settings). Folder lets you pick a destination folder in Chrome or Edge; in other browsers, images download one-by-one to Downloads instead. After export finishes, Mockup Rabbit shows an on-screen confirmation - look there if you’re unsure.

What file formats are supported? ▾

You can upload PNG and JPEG files as backgrounds or designs. Paid plans export PNG (lossless) or JPEG (adjustable quality). Use PNG if you need a transparent background.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ▾

Yes. Open Settings → Billing → Manage Billing to cancel anytime. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period - you won’t be charged again after that.

What’s your refund policy? ▾

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all recurring paid plans. If you’re not satisfied within the first 30 days, contact us at support@mockuprabbit.com. See our full Refund Policy for details.

Do you offer lifetime plans? ▾

Yes. Both Starter and Pro are available as one-time lifetime purchases - pay once and use Mockup Rabbit forever with no recurring fees. Select the “Lifetime” tab on the pricing section above to see lifetime prices.

Is my data secure? Does Mockup Rabbit store my images? ▾

Editing and export rendering happen in your browser - we don’t process your images on a server for compositing. If you use cloud save (Starter or Pro), your project snapshot - including your images - is stored on our servers so you can pick up where you left off. With Starter or Pro you can also save locally as a JSON file and keep everything on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
