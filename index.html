FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still have questions? Email us at support@mockupscripter.com

What is Mockup Scripter? ▾

Mockup Scripter is a browser-based tool that lets you composite product designs onto background images. Upload your backgrounds and designs, apply effects like blur, warp, or perspective tilt, and batch-export every combination as PNG or JPEG — all without installing any software.

Is there a free plan? ▾

Yes. The free plan lets you composite and export unlimited mockups. Exports on the free plan include a subtle watermark. Upgrade to Starter or Pro to remove watermarks and unlock advanced effects.

What's the difference between Starter and Pro? ▾

Both plans remove watermarks and unlock core effects (patterns, arc warp, perspective). Pro adds the full effects suite (fisheye, all blend modes), unlimited saved projects, copy/paste styles across mockups, and priority support. If you're a power user or agency, Pro is the better choice.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ▾

Yes. You can cancel at any time from your account settings. Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period — you won't be charged again after that.

What's your refund policy? ▾

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, contact us at support@mockupscripter.com and we'll issue a full refund. See our full Refund Policy for details.

Do you offer lifetime plans? ▾

Yes. Both Starter and Pro are available as one-time lifetime purchases — pay once and use Mockup Scripter forever with no recurring fees. Select the "Lifetime" tab on the pricing section above to see lifetime prices.

What file formats are supported? ▾

You can upload PNG and JPEG files as backgrounds or designs. Exports are available in PNG (lossless) or JPEG (adjustable quality). If you need transparent-background exports, use PNG.

Is my data secure? Does Mockup Scripter store my images? ▾

All image processing happens entirely in your browser — your images are never uploaded to our servers. We only store your account and plan information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
