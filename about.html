Mockup Scripter

A tool built out of frustration after editing thousands of mockups in Photoshop by hand.

MY GOAL: Make creating professional, realistic product mockups the LEAST time consuming part of running an eCommerce business.

Why it exists

Hey, I'm Tia TX, founder of Mockup Scripter. As an eCommerce business owner who sells on multiple platforms including Amazon, Etsy (top 1% seller) and Shopify, Mockup Scripter started as a personal problem.

If you've ever had to create dozens of product images — the same design dropped onto ten different backgrounds, exported individually, renamed, organised — you know how tedious that gets. The obvious solutions were either too expensive, required Photoshop expertise, or still involved doing the same thing over and over by hand.

I wanted something that did the repetitive part automatically. Upload your backgrounds, upload your designs, adjust a few things, hit export, get a ZIP. There's already enough to focus on for us sellers - paid ads, accounting, customer service, marketing... the list goes on. Doing a repetitive task like placing the same design on 20 different images shouldn't even be a task at all. Everything else — the effects, the warp, the patterns — came from learning what made a mockup look realistic. And the day I uploaded 125 listings in the time it would've normally taken me to upload five, I knew I had to share this with other sellers.

What it's trying to be

Mockup Scripter is for makers, sellers, and small teams who need to move fast. Etsy shop owners. Indie product designers. Developers shipping side projects. Marketing teams who don't have a designer on call. People who know exactly what they want their product to look like but don't want to spend a day in Figma to get there.

The tool runs entirely in your browser — no install, no upload of your images to a server, no subscription required to get started. You can create and export mockups on the free plan right now. The paid plans are there for when you want clean exports without a watermark, or when you want the more advanced effects.

Who's behind it

This is a solo project. I built it, I maintain it, and I read every support email personally. That means things move at a certain pace — but it also means when something is broken or confusing, you're talking directly to the person who can fix it.

If you have a feature you'd like to see, or something that's not working the way you expect, please reach out. The best ideas for what to build next have come from people actually using the tool.
